Why Protein Matters

General reasons why adequate protein intake supports an active lifestyle. Individual outcomes depend on diet, training, sleep and overall health. This is not medical advice.

Muscle Growth & Maintenance

Adequate protein supports muscle protein synthesis and helps maintain muscle mass when combined with suitable resistance exercise. A supplement can help meet daily targets when whole foods fall short.

Workout Recovery

Protein provides amino acids the body uses for muscle tissue repair after exercise. Consuming protein as part of your post-workout nutrition may support your body's natural recovery processes.

Daily Protein Support

Many people find it difficult to meet daily protein goals through meals alone. Whey protein isolate can be a convenient way to supplement dietary protein within a balanced and varied diet.

Convenient Nutrition

A protein shake offers a quick, practical option to include protein in a balanced diet and fitness routine — useful before or after workouts, or on days when a full meal is not possible.
